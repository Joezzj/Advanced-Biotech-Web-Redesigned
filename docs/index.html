03OUR TECHNOLOGY

Protection starts
at the surface.

Molecular design brings antibacterial function into the materials around us.

01

Electrostatic targeting

Many bacteria and fungi carry a negatively charged cell surface. Our positively charged antibacterial polymers are attracted to these surfaces, then disrupt membrane integrity—causing leakage of cellular contents and loss of viability.

02

Selective by design

Mammalian cell membranes are near-neutral overall, which reduces non-specific binding of cationic polymers under normal use conditions. This difference in surface charge underpins the technology’s selectivity.

03

Ready for production

The polymers exhibit high thermal stability and elevated decomposition temperatures, making them compatible with common molding and compounding processes.
